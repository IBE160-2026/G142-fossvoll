# Round 2: lead's own follow-up and spot-checks

Accessed 2026-11-19. Format: claim | source URL | publisher | pub_date | accessed | confidence | class

## Findings

- SATS Healthier & Happier Report 2025 (Nordic): among gym-goers, 59% prioritize strength training. 29% name lack of motivation as a barrier (Norway page). The Danish edition says 35% lack motivation and 17% "don't know where to start". | https://sats.no/the-healthier-and-happier-report-2025 ; https://sats.dk/the-healthier-and-happier-report-2025 | SATS | 2025 | accessed 2026-11-19 | medium (seen via search summary; operator survey) | participation / behavior
- Virke says 2025 was a very good year for the Norwegian gym industry, and that 2023 was a record profitability year. No member count was seen. | https://www.virke.no/analyse/statistikk-rapporter/treningssenterbransjen/ | Virke | 2025 | accessed 2026-11-19 | low-medium (snippet) | participation
- Liftoff ("Ranked Gym Workouts"): a free core tracker with strength ranks, streaks and leaderboards, plus an optional Pro tier. Its in-app purchases range from $9.99 to $79.99 (AppPricingLab). One source claims about 103k US ratings at 4.8 stars, and AppPricingLab shows 63.4k; the figures conflict. This is a sizeable gamified strength tracker, but its gamification is ranks and leaderboards, not a spatial path. | https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6448081563 ; https://apppricinglab.com/iap/apple/6448081563 ; https://www.sensai.fit/blog/liftoff-review | Apple App Store / AppPricingLab / SensAI | 2026 | accessed 2026-11-19 | medium | competitor
- Spot-check for spatial-path competitors (query: "gym workout app avatar walks between exercises game map"): found no app that combines an avatar, walking between exercises, and a session map. The closest are Avatone (the avatar levels up and trains alongside the user), Quest2Fitness (an RPG companion) and an unnamed calisthenics "skills map". This independently confirms D4's absence finding. | https://www.producthunt.com/products/avatone ; https://apps.apple.com/app/id6769663664 | Product Hunt / Apple | undated | accessed 2026-11-19 | medium | competitor
- Adapty's Health & Fitness benchmark (2026): install-to-trial 9.5%, trial-to-paid 42.2%, first renewal 67.7%. Health & Fitness leads one-year LTV per install at $1.21. From these, install-to-paid comes to roughly 4%. This is the same order of magnitude as RevenueCat's median of 2.9% from a different publisher and dataset, so it verifies the D1 conversion claim. Caveat: Adapty's 2025 report used a different method. | https://adapty.io/blog/health-fitness-app-subscription-benchmarks/ ; https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions-report/ | Adapty | 2026 | accessed 2026-11-19 | medium-high | benchmark
