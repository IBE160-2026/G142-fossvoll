### Behavior
- A 2026 survey article (Polish Journal of Sport and Tourism) found that 80.6% of respondents used fitness apps but only 1.4% saw them as equivalent to a personal trainer. Respondents valued apps for progress tracking, diet monitoring and low cost. Only the search snippet was seen, not the full paper, and the sample is likely Polish students. | https://reference-global.com/article/10.2478/pjst-2026-0012 | De Gruyter / Reference Global | 2026 | accessed 2026-11-19 | low (snippet only) | behavior
- UK prices: apps about £5–30/month, personal trainer about £30–60+ per session. The source is an app vendor. | https://www.findyouredge.app/news/personal-trainer-vs-fitness-app-uk-2026 | FindYourEdge (vendor blog) | 2026 | accessed 2026-11-19 | low | behavior/pricing

## Leads worth chasing
- RevenueCat 2026 PDF: H&F renewal and retention rates (annual first-renewal, monthly churn) and to settle the annual-vs-monthly mix. Retention by category was not on the web page.
- Full text of pjst-2026-0012, which may contain WTP and beginner segmentation.
- RevenueCat Sub Club podcast with Guillem Ros (Hevy). It may give a free-to-paid ratio and price-test results: https://revenuecat.com/blog/growth/guillem-ros-hevy-podcast
- The multiple SKUs per tier on Strong, Fitbod and Hevy suggest active price testing. Adapty's paywall library may show which variant is live.
- Alpha Progression, JEFIT, Liftoff (gamified, relevant to the game-like layer) and Ladder prices were not collected because the budget ran out. These are a priority for the next round.
- Sensor Tower or Appfigures revenue estimates for Hevy, Strong and Fitbod.
- Adapty "State of In-App Subscriptions" H&F numbers, as a second benchmark publisher to triangulate RevenueCat.
